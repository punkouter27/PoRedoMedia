AFTER

A third place: Create Movie

PoRedoMediaCreateCreate MovieLibrary

  • Films: what it's about, films from the Library, then the whole video in one go, or a script to look over first.
  • Voice-over: clips in order; edit, play, record voices, generate pictures, put your own film on a clip.
  • Video: render with chapters, subtitles and thumbnail; download, add to Library, upload to YouTube; upright teasers.
